1. End-to-End Data Plane — Five Different Meanings of “Batch”

The key distinction: the DataLoader batch, rollout submission batch, vLLM scheduler batch, replay prompt-group batch, and FSDP micro-batch are different objects. vLLM never receives one dense 64 × 17 tensor; it receives independent asynchronous requests and continuously forms its own execution batches.

2. Source Row → Generation Request — Why Two Representations Exist

Generation does not consume the padded dataset tensor directly. The singleton item retains both an HF/FSDP-friendly padded representation and the structured messages/raw audio needed to construct the model-specific vLLM multimodal request. After generation, final training tensors are rebuilt around the exact response.

4. Inside vLLM — Routing, Continuous Batching, and Abort/Resume

Why continuous batching matters: requests arrive and finish independently. Each engine schedules up to 16 active sequences under a 28,672-token ceiling, so short requests free slots immediately. A policy sync interrupts rather than drains generation; FullyAsyncLLMServerClient resumes partial output against the refreshed policy and records the version span.
