# Number-Use Scenarios in Earnings Calls

This reference summarizes 72 common ways numbers appear in earnings-call
meetings. Each scenario includes a display-text example and the corresponding
spoken form, making the list suitable for ASR, text normalization, and synthetic
data design.

Scenarios 1-30 were derived from official Microsoft earnings-call transcripts.
Scenarios 31-72 extend that taxonomy with illustrative examples covering major
currencies, decimal percentages, dates, times, and durations. Earnings-22 was
not used.

## Coverage Summary

- Financial values: revenue, expenses, earnings per share, margins, cash flow,
  capital expenditure, leases, backlog, taxes, and shareholder returns.
- Comparisons and forecasts: growth rates, percentage points, guidance ranges,
  thresholds, qualitative bands, foreign exchange, and constant currency.
- Scale metrics: customers, users, seats, regions, datacenters, data volume, and
  multiplicative changes.
- Numeric forms: integers, decimals, negatives, fractions, percentages, basis
  points, ranges, approximations, inequalities, units, and large-number scales.
- International forms: 20 major currencies with symbols or ISO-style labels.
- Temporal forms: calendar dates, numeric dates, fiscal periods, clock times,
  time zones, ranges, and durations.
